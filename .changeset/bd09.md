BD-09 (Baidu): `BD09` and `bd09(options)`, a `Geographic` for WGS84 ↔ BD-09 (standalone or as a projection's `geographic`) that also converts GCJ-02 ↔ BD-09 directly (`toGcj02`, `fromGcj02` and their interleaved and point forms). Baidu's published offset over GCJ-02, as in gcoord and coordtransform, applied everywhere; `outsideChina` governs the GCJ-02 step. The forward is within an ulp of a 40-digit reference; the inverse takes two Newton steps and is within an ulp of the exact inverse, where gcoord's closed form is off by up to 2e-6° (0.2 m). A wasm context's `bd09()` runs the offset over its wasm GCJ-02.
